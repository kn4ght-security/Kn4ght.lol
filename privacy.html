Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 3, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Kn4ght Security may handle information when you use the bot or its related services.

2. Information We May Process

Depending on the features used, Kn4ght Security may process Discord identifiers such as user IDs, server IDs, channel IDs, role IDs, and other information necessary for the bot to operate.

3. Verification Protection

When verification features are used, Kn4ght Security may process information necessary to determine whether a member has completed the required verification process.

4. CAPTCHA Protection

CAPTCHA protection may process information required to determine whether a verification attempt was successfully completed. CAPTCHA providers may process information according to their own privacy policies.

5. Alt Detection

Alt detection may use available account or Discord-related information to identify potentially suspicious alternate accounts. Detection results are not guaranteed to be completely accurate.

6. Lockdown and Moderation

When authorized administrators use lockdown or related security features, Kn4ght Security may process the Discord information required to perform those actions.

7. How Information Is Used

Information may be used to provide security features, perform verification, detect suspicious activity, maintain the bot, troubleshoot problems, and improve the service.

8. Data Retention

Information should only be retained for as long as necessary for the relevant bot functionality, security, or legitimate operational purposes. Actual retention periods may depend on the feature and how the bot is configured.

9. Third-Party Services

Kn4ght Security may rely on Discord and other third-party services for certain functionality. Those services may process information under their own privacy policies.

10. Data Security

Reasonable measures may be used to protect information handled by Kn4ght Security. However, no online service can guarantee complete security.

11. Data Requests

If you have a question about information handled by Kn4ght Security or want to request deletion of applicable data, contact the Kn4ght Security development team through the official support channel.

12. Children's Privacy

Kn4ght Security is a Discord service and should only be used in accordance with Discord's applicable age requirements and policies.

13. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the bot's features, data practices, or services change. The newest version will be published on this page.

14. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact the Kn4ght Security development team through the official support channel.
